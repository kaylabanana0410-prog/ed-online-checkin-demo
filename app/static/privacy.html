Privacy information

DRAFT for a demonstration with synthetic data. It is not a legal notice and has not been reviewed under Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA) or approved by any hospital.

What this form collects

Your name, date of birth, phone, optional email, your answers about your symptoms, whether you provided a health card, and your consent choices.

What it does not keep

Health card photos are read once and deleted straight away. Health card numbers are not stored.

How it is used

Hospital staff use it to register you faster. Online check-in does not hold a place in the queue and is not a medical assessment.

AI summary (optional)

If you agree, a computer program may summarize your symptom answers for staff. It does not diagnose, decide urgency, or see your name or contact details. You can say no and still check in.

How long it is kept

Demonstration records are deleted after a set period (30 days by default). A hospital would set its own retention rules.
